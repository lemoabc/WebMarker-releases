# Privacy Policy for Web Marker

**Last updated: May 11, 2026**

## Overview

Web Marker is a browser extension that allows you to highlight and annotate text on any webpage. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

## Data Collection

**Web Marker does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal data to any server.**

All your annotation data is stored entirely on your local device using the browser's built-in storage API (`chrome.storage.local`). No information ever leaves your browser.

## What We Don't Do

- We do NOT collect browsing history
- We do NOT track your activity
- We do NOT use analytics or telemetry
- We do NOT share any data with third parties
- We do NOT use cookies
- We do NOT require account registration

## Permissions Explained

| Permission | Purpose |
|------------|---------|
| `storage` | Save your annotations locally on your device |
| `activeTab` | Access the current page content to enable text highlighting |

These are the minimum permissions required for the extension to function. We do not request any broad or sensitive permissions.

## Data Storage

- All annotations are stored locally in your browser
- Data persists across browser sessions on your device only
- You can export your data at any time via the extension popup
- You can delete all data by removing the extension

## Open Source

The extension's release builds are available on this repository. You can inspect the code to verify our privacy claims.

## Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above.

## Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on this GitHub repository.
